CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0

RETURN POLICY

Customers may return eligible products within 30 calendar days
from the date of delivery.

Returned products must be unused, undamaged, and in their original
packaging.

Footwear may be tried indoors for fit but must not show signs
of outdoor wear.

Items returned after 30 days are not normally eligible for return.

Exceptions may be considered for defective products covered by
the warranty. These cases must be escalated to a human support agent.

Customers must provide an order number before a return can be processed.


REFUND POLICY

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing normally begins after the returned item has been
received and inspected.

The AI support assistant cannot approve or issue refunds.

Refund exceptions must be escalated to a human support agent.


DELIVERY POLICY

Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking link provided
in their shipping confirmation.

If a package has not arrived by the estimated delivery date,
the customer should verify the tracking information first.

If tracking shows the package as delivered but the customer has
not received it, the case must be escalated to human support.

The AI assistant does not have direct access to live carrier
tracking unless a tracking tool is explicitly connected.


ACCOUNT SECURITY POLICY

Suspected unauthorized account access is considered a sensitive
security issue.

The AI assistant must not request passwords, payment card numbers,
security codes, or other authentication secrets.

Customers reporting unauthorized access should be advised to
change their password using the official account settings and
contact human customer support immediately.

Sensitive security incidents must be escalated.


VIP POLICY

There is currently no special VIP return policy based on the
number of products purchased.

All customers follow the standard return policy unless an approved
company policy explicitly states otherwise.
